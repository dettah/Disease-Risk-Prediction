DISEASE RISK PREDICTION AI

Purpose:
Estimate type 2 diabetes risk from patient measurements using supervised machine learning.

Input:
Pregnancies, glucose, blood pressure, skin thickness, insulin, BMI,
diabetes pedigree function, and age.

Output:
Model probability, risk category, priority, and a non-diagnostic recommendation.

Model:
Logistic Regression with StandardScaler and class balancing.

Important:
The output is a machine-learning risk estimate, not a medical diagnosis.
